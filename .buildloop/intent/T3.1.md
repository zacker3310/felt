# Intent T3.1: POV stroke challenge (3D, down the cue)

**Originator:** Zac, 2026-10-25, in his words:

> What do you think about a 3-D shot mode?? Like POV aiming down the cue and having the cue ball
> right there could be cool

> And yeah that 3-D first player POV mode is a challenge mode where the putting stroke like from
> puttX how you can miss left and right and shit matters
> We will build that later so put a task plan together for that build okay

## What is wanted

- A challenge mode played from the shooter's eyes: the camera down on the cue, behind the cue
  ball, the object ball and pocket ahead in perspective.
- The stroke is a skill: like puttX's putting stroke, a real stroke gesture whose quality decides
  the shot. A crooked stroke misses left or right; tempo and follow-through matter. The top-down
  game's bar is a perfect machine; this mode is not.
- It is a challenge mode, not the default way to play.

## Read into the ask

- "Like from puttX": port puttX's stroke model (gesture capture, how path deviation and tempo
  map to start-line error and pace), retuned for a cue: a cue stroke is straight back and through,
  so the stroke's sideways wobble and its angle at contact become cue-ball direction error (and
  unintended side english), tempo becomes speed error.
- Physics, rules and determinism stay. 3D is a renderer and an input mode on top of the same
  flat sim: the stroke produces `{ angle, power, spin }` like the bar does, then the usual
  `strike`/`stepTable` run. Same shot, same result.
- Phone first (iPhone 18 Pro Max, landscape likely best for POV), no libraries, offline.

## Open (decide at spec time)

- Whether POV also becomes an optional aiming view in normal games (the earlier idea), or stays
  challenge-only.
- Challenge structure: ladder of shots with a score per shot (distance from perfect line, pot or
  miss, position), or reuse the trick-shot list with a POV twist.
