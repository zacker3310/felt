# Intent: a better menu

**id:** T3.2   **originator:** Zac   **date:** 2026-10-25
**status:** accepted

In his words:

> Research games like this and implement a better menu and such

## Problem
The menu is one long settings form: the brand, an Opponent toggle, the game cards, the solo
cards, then table size, pockets, cloth, bumper shape, aim guide, auto aim and sound, then a long
How to play block. On his iPhone (440x956, and 956x440 on its side) it scrolls past a screen
either way up. Settings weigh as much as playing; there is nothing to look at but small ball icons.

## Desired outcome
A home screen like a shipped mobile pool game (8 Ball Pool, Pool Break, Pro Pool, Billiards City):
- one tap to play again; big art-forward tiles for the three games; a short setup step per game
  (opponent, then only that game's table) with a big Break button;
- settings, stats and how to play behind their own buttons;
- named CPU opponents for the three levels, a daily challenge with a streak, a record of games;
- fits one screen on his phone either way up; the pause menu keeps Resume obvious.

## Who is affected
Zac and whoever he hands the phone to; the home-screen web app on an iPhone 18 Pro Max.

## Constraints
One offline `index.html`, no libraries, no network, no accounts or money (take the genre's
structure, not its monetisation). The sim stays DOM-free and clock-free; only the power bar
shoots. Contrast 4.5:1 for text. No em dashes.

## Open questions
Resolved in `.buildloop/spec/T3.2-menu.md`: opponent names, how the daily shot is picked, what
counts toward the streak and the stats.

## Not doing
Online play, accounts, coins, cue shop, levels to unlock, leaderboards.
