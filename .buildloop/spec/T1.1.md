# Spec T1.1: felt v1

Intent: [[.buildloop/intent/T1.1|T1.1 intent]]. Design source for the physics: [[puttX/SPEC|puttX]].

## Shape

- One `index.html`: style, markup, and one `<script id="game">`. No libraries, no network,
  works from `file://` and offline. 2D canvas, top-down table, drawn in-page.
- `// #region sim` ... `// #endregion sim` holds physics, rules and the CPU. It uses no DOM,
  no `Math.random`, no clocks. `tests/sim.cjs` loads it into a Node `vm`, as puttX does.
- `package.json` is test tooling only: `eslint` 10.11.0 and `playwright-core` 1.63.0, the same
  pins as puttX.

## Physics (ported from puttX, extended)

- Metres and seconds. 9 ft table: 2.54 x 1.27 m playing surface, ball radius 0.028575 m.
- Fixed step `SIM_DT = 1/1000`. Max cue speed 7 m/s, so no ball moves more than 7 mm a step
  (under a quarter of a radius): no tunnelling.
- Kept from puttX: `closestOnSeg`, the `bounce` reflection (restitution plus tangential keep,
  speed cap), segment walls (the cushions), rolling friction as a constant deceleration,
  `STOP_V` rest detection, `MAX_SIM_T` timeout.
- New: ball-ball collisions (equal mass, restitution 0.94, positional de-overlap) in a fixed
  pair order; pockets as gaps in the cushion line (a centre past the nose line is potted);
  vertical spin (follow/draw, at most 1.6 natural rolls) that the cloth converts toward natural
  roll; at contact the cue ball leaves at 5/7 of its post-impact velocity plus 2/7 of the spin's
  surface velocity along its incoming line, never faster than it arrived.
- Same shot from the same layout gives a byte-identical result.

## Rules

- **8-ball.** 15 balls racked with the 8 in the centre, a solid and a stripe in the back
  corners. Break from behind the head string. Table is open until someone legally pots a ball
  after the break; the first ball potted sets the groups. Legal shot: first contact with a ball
  of your group (any non-8 on an open table, the 8 once your group is clear), then a pot or any
  ball to a rail. Fouls give ball in hand anywhere. Pot the 8 legally with your group cleared to
  win; pot it early or scratch on it and you lose. 8 on the break is re-spotted. Any pocket counts
  (no calling).
- **9-ball.** 1-9 in a diamond, 1 at the apex on the foot spot, 9 in the centre. First contact
  must be the lowest ball on the table; then a pot or a rail. A legal 9 wins, on any shot
  including the break. A 9 potted on a foul is re-spotted. Three fouls in a row lose. Fouls
  give ball in hand anywhere.
- Both: a break scratch gives ball in hand behind the head string. Potting a legal ball keeps
  the turn.

## Play

- Menu: 8-ball or 9-ball; two players or vs CPU.
- Aim: mouse points the cue (fine-tune with arrow keys, shift for coarse). Touch: drag on the
  table. Guide line shows first contact, the ghost ball and the object ball's path.
- Shoot: pull down the power bar and release, or with a mouse press on the table and pull back
  along the cue. Space shoots at the bar's last power.
- Spin: tap the cue-ball widget for follow (top) or draw (bottom).
- Ball in hand: drag the cue ball to a legal spot.
- HUD: whose turn, groups or the ball on, foul and result messages, rematch.
- Sounds synthesised: ball clicks, cushion thuds, pocket drops.

## Acceptance criteria (each has a check)

1. `npm test` runs every suite; all pass.
2. `npm run lint` reports 0 findings.
3. Physics: a straight shot pots into a corner and a side pocket; cushions reflect; a full stun
   hit stops the cue ball and sends the object ball on; follow goes forward, draw comes back;
   kinetic energy never rises in a collision without a cue ball, and at a cue-ball contact the cue ball never speeds up and the table gains no more than the spin's energy; aimed shots with spin never move a ball more than 7 mm a step; 300 fuzzed shots and breaks never produce a NaN,
   a ball off the table and not potted, an overlap at rest, or a timeout.
4. Determinism: the same shot twice gives identical JSON; the live page's result equals
   `simulateShot` for the same shot.
5. Rules: each 8-ball and 9-ball rule above has a case in `rules-check`.
6. CPU: pots a hanging ball; CPU vs CPU finishes a full game of each kind with a winner.
7. Page: boots with no console errors and no network beyond itself; menu starts both games;
   a shot through the UI changes the turn state; no horizontal scroll at 390 px wide; buttons at
   least 44 px.
