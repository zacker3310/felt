# Spec: T2.1 overhaul

**id:** T2.1   **intent:** .buildloop/intent/T2.1.md
**status:** accepted

## Summary

Rebuild felt into a game that plays like pool. Five workstreams run in parallel, each owning a
disjoint part of `index.html` and of `tests/`. They meet only at the contract below. Anything
not in the contract is private to its owner and may change freely.

## Ownership

| Stream | Owns in `index.html` | Owns in `tests/` |
|---|---|---|
| **P** physics | sim region 1a-1d (constants, table, collisions, motion), `spinOf` | `physics-check.cjs` |
| **C** CPU + rules | sim region 1e-1f (rules, CPU) | `rules-check.cjs`, `ai-check.cjs` |
| **V** canvas | section 3 view (except `layout()`), section 4 render, `draw()`, `guide()`, `drawCue()`, new `onHits()`/`onPot()` | new `render-check.cjs` if wanted |
| **U** DOM + UX | `<style>`, markup, `layout()`, section 5 play (state machine, input, `frame()`, `think()`), section 6 hud, section 7 boot, `eslint.config.js` | `play-check.cjs`, `boot-check.cjs`, `lib.cjs` |
| **A** audio | section 2 audio | none |

Stay out of other streams' code. Where you need something from another stream that does not
exist yet, add the smallest stub that satisfies the contract, mark it `/* STUB(T2.1) */`, and
say so in your report: the integrator replaces it with the owner's version.

## Contract

### Sim exports (P provides, everyone reads)

- Table: `TL = 2.54`, `TW = 1.27`, `BALL_R = 0.028575`, `HEAD_X`, `FOOT` unchanged.
- `CUSH`: array of segments `[ax, ay, bx, by]`, every collision surface of the cushions,
  jaws included. Inner edge of the playing area.
- `CUSHION_POLYS`: array of polygons `[[x, y], ...]` in table coordinates: the rubber
  cushions as V should paint them. Their inner edges coincide with `CUSH`; they extend outward
  to the rail (`y <= 0`, `y >= TW`, `x <= 0`, `x >= TL` side).
- `POCKETS`: array of 6 `[x, y]` drop centres, order: 0 head-top corner `(0,0)`, 1 top side,
  2 foot-top corner, 3 head-bottom corner `(0,TW)`, 4 bottom side, 5 foot-bottom corner.
- `POCKET_R`: array of 6 drop radii (m), for drawing the hole.
- `POCKET_AIM`: array of 6 `[x, y]` aim points: the middle of each mouth. CPU aims here.
- Ball objects: `{ n, x, y, vx, vy, on, ...private }`. `n` 0 is the cue ball. Private fields
  (angular velocity etc.) are P's; nobody else reads them. Balls at rest have `vx = vy = 0`.
- `spinOf(s)`: normalises a spin argument. A number `s` means `{ x: 0, y: s }`; an object is
  `{ x, y }` clamped so `x*x + y*y <= 1`. `x` is side english (+ = right of centre as seen by the
  shooter), `y` is vertical (+ = follow, - = draw). `{0,0}` is a centre-ball hit.
- `strike(tab, angle, power, spin)`, `stepTable(tab)`, `resultOf(tab)`, `newTable(balls)`,
  `simulateShot(balls, { angle, power, spin })`: same signatures as v1; `spin` goes through
  `spinOf`. `power` in [0, 1].
- `tab.hits`: array of `[kind, intensity]`, kind in `'cue' | 'ball' | 'rail' | 'jaw' | 'pocket'`,
  intensity roughly impact speed in m/s (pocket: drop speed). `frame()` drains it every frame.
- `tab.potted` (ball numbers in pot order), `tab.pocketOf[n]` (pocket index), `tab.first`
  (first ball the cue ball touched, or null), `tab.railAfter` (a ball hit a cushion after first
  contact), `tab.end` in `'rest' | 'timeout'`.
- `tab.crossed` / `resultOf(tab).crossed`: the cue ball's centre reached `x >= HEAD_X` at or
  before first contact (fix round). `judge` reads it for the kitchen rule.
- `powerFor(v)`: exact inverse of `speedOf`. `POCKET_LIP` (m): a ball coming to rest within this
  of a hole's edge drops; moving balls drop at `POCKET_R`.
- Determinism: no `Math.random`, no clocks, no DOM in the sim region. Same input, same JSON.

### Rules and CPU exports (C provides)

- `newGame(kind)`, `legalTargets(g)`, `judge(g, r)`, `canPlace`, `placeCue`, `spot`: same
  signatures as v1. `judge` never mutates `g`.
- `cpuSearch(g, seed, level)`: generator, yields after each simulation, returns
  `{ angle, power, spin: {x, y}, place: [x, y] | null }`. `level` in
  `'easy' | 'medium' | 'hard'`, default `'medium'`. `cpuShot(g, seed, level)` runs it to the end.
- A `hard` CPU beats a `medium` CPU over 20 seeded games of each kind; `easy` misses noticeably.
- `judge` reads `r.crossed` (P: the cue ball's centre reached `HEAD_X` before or at first
  contact). In hand behind the head string, a first contact is legal when that ball lies past
  the string or `r.crossed === true`; a result without the field is judged on position alone.
  With no legal ball past the string any legal hit counts (stands in for the WPA/BCA spot request).
- `verdict.twoFouls` (fix round): 9-ball, the player to shoot next is on two fouls in a row; the
  UI may warn. `game.dry`: shots in a row with nothing potted (the CPU's loop breaker reads it).

### Page globals (U declares in section 5, V's `draw()` reads)

`game`, `tab`, `state` (`'menu' | 'place' | 'aim' | 'stroke' | 'rolling' | 'thinking' | 'over'`),
`aimAngle`, `power`, `cueSpin` (`{x, y}`), `pull` (non-null while a drag-to-shoot is in
progress), `placeAt` (`[x, y]` or null), `cpuPlan`, `stroke` (`null` or `{ t0, power }`: set when
a shot is committed; for `STROKE_MS` the cue is drawn moving forward, then `shoot()` runs the
physics), `settings` (`{ guide: 'full' | 'short' | 'off', ... }`), `view` (`{ k, ox, oy, portrait }`),
`dpr`.

- `layout()` (U) sizes the canvas, sets `view` and `dpr`, then calls `tableImg = drawTableImage()` (V).
- `frame(now)` (U) steps physics, then calls `onHits(tab.hits, now)` (V, visual effects) and
  `sfx(kind, v)` (A) per hit, then for each ball newly off the table `onPot(n, k, from, now)` (V).
  Then `draw(now)` (V).
- `toScreen(x, y)` and `toTable(sx, sy)` stay in section 3 and stay on `window.felt`.

### Audio (A provides)

`audioOn()` (call from a user gesture), `sfx(kind, v)` for every hit kind above,
`muted` (let), plus `sfx('foul')`, `sfx('turn')`, `sfx('win')` for U to call. No network, no
audio files: synthesised with Web Audio.

### Test hook (`window.felt`, U)

Keeps `state`, `game`, `cpu`, `power`, `aim`, `start`, `shoot(angle, power, spin)`, `toScreen`,
`speed`, `forceWin`, `lastResult`. `shoot` goes straight to physics (no stroke delay) so tests
stay fast. May add more.

## Requirements by stream

**P physics.** Real ball dynamics: linear and angular velocity per ball, a sliding phase with
cloth friction that turns into natural roll, rolling resistance, spin decay about the vertical.
Cue strike from tip offset (`spinOf`, up to about half a radius) gives follow, draw, stun, and
side english with squirt. Ball-ball contact with collision-induced throw (cut and english).
Cushions with restitution and english that changes the rebound angle. Real pocket geometry:
angled jaws, mouths near WPA sizes (corner about 4.5-5 in, side about 5-5.5 in), drop when the
centre clears the shelf, rattles on bad hits. No tunnelling. Fuzz suite stays green; the whole
physics suite runs in under 40 s.

**C CPU + rules.** Search that simulates rather than trusts closed-form power: candidate
pots into `POCKET_AIM`, a few powers and spins each, scored on pot plus next-shot position
(the best follow-up's quality), safeties when nothing pots, ball in hand placement that leaves
an easy shot. Three levels by search breadth and aim noise. Keep a game under 300 shots and the
ai suite under 60 s.

**V canvas.** A table that looks like a table: cushions painted from `CUSHION_POLYS` with a
lit bevel, pockets with leather/liner and depth, rails with wood grain and sights, cloth with
nap and light falloff. Balls: cached rendering, crisp numbers, specular, soft contact
shadows, roll that matches motion. Aim guide: ghost ball, object-ball line to where it goes,
cue-ball path after contact bent by follow/draw, length by `settings.guide`. Cue that pulls
back with power and strikes forward during `stroke`. Pot animation into the actual pocket. A
pocketed-ball tray. Light hit effects from `onHits` (no confetti). Steady 60 fps with 16 balls on
a mid phone (do not rebuild ImageData per ball per frame).

**U DOM + UX.** Portrait phone: the table fills the width, controls sit in a strip that does
not steal table space. Landscape: table as big as fits. Power: drag back from the cue ball (or
anywhere on the table) along the aim line, with the stroke shown on the cue; a power control
that does not sit half full in red when idle. Spin: a 2D english widget (drag a tip dot on a
cue-ball face), reset by double tap. Touch aim: tap aims absolutely, drag rotates relative with
fine control. Keyboard: arrows, shift, space, `R` reset spin. Menu: game, opponent, CPU level,
guide setting, remembered in `localStorage`. HUD: players, groups or ball on, pocketed counts,
clear foul/turn banners. Game over: winner, why, shots, fouls, rematch. 44 px targets, 4.5:1 text
contrast, no horizontal scroll at 390 px.

**A audio.** Ball clack with pitch and level by impact, cushion thump, jaw rattle, pocket
drop with a roll down the gully, cue tip, foul, turn and win cues. Gentle, never clipping.
Limiter on the master.

## Acceptance criteria

AC1. `npm test` passes, every suite | run it
AC2. `npm run lint` 0 findings | run it
AC3. Every v1 physics check still holds or has a stricter replacement | physics-check
AC4. Side english: right english off a straight-in cushion hit sends the cue ball right; throw
moves a cut object ball measurably off the ghost-ball line | physics-check
AC5. Pocket: a ball rolled hard into the jaw at a bad angle can rattle out; a straight ball
drops | physics-check
AC6. Live roll equals `simulateShot` byte for byte | play-check
AC7. CPU levels ordered: hard beats medium over seeded games | ai-check
AC8. No network, no console errors, 44 px targets, no horizontal scroll at 390 px | boot-check
AC9. Phone portrait: the table is at least 85% of the viewport width | boot-check

## Rejected alternatives

- Splitting `index.html` into modules: breaks the one-file, `file://`, offline contract.
- A physics library: no libraries, and determinism is a test.
- 50 agents: one 1000-line file does not partition 50 ways. Five owners and a contract.
