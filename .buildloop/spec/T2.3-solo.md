# Spec: T2.3 practice and trick shots

**id:** T2.3   **status:** accepted
Extends `.buildloop/spec/T2.1.md` and `T2.2.md`; their ownership and contract still hold, with these additions.

## Contract additions

- **Shot result fields (sim, 1c/1d).** `resultOf(tab)` also returns, all deterministic:
  `pocketOf` (ball number to pocket index), `rails` (ball number to cushion-nose contacts over
  the whole shot; jaw facings not counted), `kick` (the cue ball's cushion contacts before its
  first ball contact, `null` when it touched no ball) and `contacts` (each pair of balls `[lo, hi]`
  that touched, once, in the order they first met). `tab.met` is private bookkeeping.
- **Game mode.** `game.mode` is `undefined` in 8-ball and 9-ball, `'practice'` or `'trick'` in a
  solo session. With a mode set, `legalTargets(g)` is every object ball on the table.
- **Practice (sim, 1e+).** `newPractice(layout, seed)`, layout `'8' | '9' | 'scatter' | 'empty'`;
  `game.hand` is the ball-in-hand toggle. `practiceJudge(g, r)` (never mutates `g`): no fouls,
  `turn` stays 0, never a winner, potted balls stay down, `inHand` is `'table'` after a scratch or
  with `hand` on, else null; `verdict.cleared` when no object ball is left. `scatter(seed, count)`,
  `canDrop(g, x, y)`, `addBall(g, x, y)` (lowest free number 1..15), `removeBall(g, n)`.
- **Trick shots (sim, 1g).** `TRICKS`: `{ id, title, goal, balls(T), check(r, P), zone?, pocket? }`,
  layouts in table fractions and pocket-relative helpers, so they fit both sizes. `trickGame(c)`,
  `trickBalls(c)`, `trickPoints(c)`, `trickJudge(c, r)` -> `{ ok, why }` (a scratch always fails),
  `trickStars(attempts)` (3 / 2 within three / 1), `TRICK_SOLS[id][cfg]` (`cfg` `'7r' | '9r' | '7g' | '9g'`,
  `[angle, power, side, vertical]`), `trickSol(c)` for the current table.
  `tools/trick-solver.cjs` regenerates `tests/trick-solutions.json`; the page's `TRICK_SOLS` must
  equal it (solo-check).
- **Page (section 5s).** `solo` (the session or null), `settleSolo()` (settle() hands solo shots
  to it), `drawSolo()` (draw() calls it after the hint: the zone ring and the named pocket),
  `hudSolo()` / `hudSoloMenu()` / `syncSoloMenu()` (called first by hudChip, hudMenu, syncMenu).
  Undo snapshots are taken in `shoot()` (practice only). Trick progress: localStorage `felt.tricks`,
  `{ id: stars }`. Test hook: `felt.practice`, `felt.trick`, `felt.undo`, `felt.resetRack`,
  `felt.toggleHand`, `felt.setEdit`, `felt.editAt`, `felt.showMe`, `felt.solo`, `felt.trickBest`,
  `felt.lastTrick`.
- **Menu.** Play (opponent, CPU level, `#playRow`: 8-ball, 9-ball; new game kinds add their card
  here), Solo (`#soloRow`: Practice, Trick shots), then Table and Aim and sound.

## Acceptance

AC1. Practice: racks, scatter, empty; no fouls; undo restores the layout and the same shot gives
the same result; ball in hand toggle; reset; free-table add and remove by touch | solo-check
AC2. Live practice roll equals simulateShot byte for byte | solo-check
AC3. At least 12 trick shots; each goal passes on its stored solution and fails on a miss; a
solver search finds a solution for each on the 7 ft regulation table | solo-check
AC4. Stars by attempts, persisted, shown in the list and the menu | solo-check
AC5. 44 px targets, 4.5:1 text on the new screens, both orientations; all suites, lint 0, no em dashes.
