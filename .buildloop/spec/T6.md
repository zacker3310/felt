Intent: [[.buildloop/intent/T6|T6]]. Evidence: [[.buildloop/spec/T6-audit-pool|pool audit]],
[[.buildloop/spec/T6-audit-snooker-carom|snooker and carom audit]], [[.buildloop/spec/T6-research-looks|looks research]].

Four streams, run one at a time on `main`, each green and pushed before the next:
T6.1 rules, T6.2 looks, T6.3 trick shots, T6.4 career.

Standing constraints, all streams:

- Physics, shot controls and determinism do not change. A rule may read a new fact from a result; it may not change how any ball moves. `play-check`'s live-vs-sim and `tests/level-cue.cjs` stay green untouched.
- Saves load: settings, record, stars, career slots.
- A test changes only when the audit shows it pinned the wrong behaviour. Every flipped check is named in the commit message with its rule number.
- `tests/` and `npm run lint` green before each push. CLAUDE.md's bullets updated with the code. No em dashes.

## T6.1 rules

Authorities: WPA Rules of Play and Regulations 2026-10-21, WPA Rules of Heyball 2025-08-16, WPBSA Official
Rules revised September 2024, UMB World Rules of Carom Billiard 1989 with CEB 2021 (best available, no
current primary text is published). Rule numbers cited in code comments.

Already written, uncommitted in the tree, untested: heyball calls nothing (s11), table 1260 mm, source WPA 2025.

### Fix: every CONFIRMED item in both audits, with these decisions

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Pool 4, heyball s10.4 simultaneous contact | **Cut.** A cross-group tie inside one 1/1200 s step with both potted is vanishingly rare. Recorded as a known gap in the ruleset's comment. |
| Pool 5, ball in hand with all legal balls behind the string | A choice, not automatic: option `kitchenSpot`, choices `spot` (the legal ball nearest the head string goes to the foot spot, WPA 1.6) and `play`, on WPA sets and heyball. House rules keep today's guard. |
| Pool 10, 10-ball obvious shots | `10-wpa` gets `obvious: true`. Delete `10-house` (now identical); a stored `rules10: '10-house'` falls to the default through the existing validation. |
| Pool 13, three-ball rule | Count object balls past the head string **at rest** plus pots, from `r.balls`. Reg 16 allows "center (or resting point)". No sim change. |
| Pool 6, frozen ball | May touch `stepTable`'s rail bookkeeping only. Motion byte-identical. Keep the hot path's allocation at zero. |
| Pool 17, 18, 24 | Fix. They're low, and cheap. |
| Snooker 12, re-spotted black | Seeded draw (`rng`, from the game's shots and score), winner chooses through `game.option`, kind `snkBlack`, with its own `OPT_WORDS`. CPU plays it. |
| Carom 5, frozen balls | The foul for playing into a touching ball, and the `respot` option. **Cut** the foul for driving into a frozen cushion: it needs the stroke's direction in the result. Recorded as a known gap. |

### Ambiguous items, readings adopted

- Heyball 12.1 over 10.3: first 1, only the 9 potted: table stays open, ball stays down, turn passes.
- Heyball 6(f) foul list: one option. 6(g): no contact on the break gives `['rebreak','break']`, no ball in hand.
- Heyball 20(c): the 8 off the table on the break loses the rack, heyball only.
- WPA 4.3(f) "re-breaking": the opponent breaks.
- 9-ball: Reg 16 over 5.3(c).
- Heyball `callScope`: Settings' 8-ball-only mode does not make it call. Only the custom All mode does.
- Snooker's ambiguous items: leave as they are.
- Not modelled, and said so in a comment: soft-break escalation (6a), intentional fouls (18), stalemates. All need a referee's judgement.

## T6.3 trick shots

Evidence: [[.buildloop/spec/T6-research-tricks-career|research]]. Structure from the WPA Artistic Pool 2025 program; setups are original, or generic classics, in felt's own words.

- Seven disciplines, WPA's eight without Special Arts (its shots need re-hits and moving balls): Trick and Fancy, Draw, Follow, Bank and Kick, Stroke, Jump, Masse. Each has difficulty slots 6 to 10.
- Scoring, WPA's: full value on the first attempt, minus 1, minus 2, else 0. Best score per shot; a discipline is worth 40, the whole program 280. Free practice stays unlimited and unscored.
- **This fixes the real weakness:** `trickTries` never resets, so one early miss caps a shot's stars for life. Attempts become per run, and a new run can always beat the old score.
- `scratchOk` on a challenge: WPA allows a scratch unless the shot says otherwise.
- Saves: new `felt.trickScore.v2`. Migrated once from `felt.tricks`: 3 stars is full value, 2 is minus 1, 1 is minus 2; never lowers a score. Old keys keep being written (solo-check pins them).
- Rename `jboot` and `jknievel`'s displayed names, which carry real people's names. Ids stay.
- New shots: the research's table. Each must pass `tools/trick-solver.cjs` on every table setting with the existing margin (at least 20 makes where sweeps are used), or it is dropped or moved. Drop outright: Frozen Rail Cut, Three-Corner Tour, Double Bank. Rework first, as margins are thin: Side Draw, Double Hop, Bend Back, Split, Diagonal Draw, Ring Draw, Boomerang Masse. A program slot with no solver-proven shot stays empty; never ship an unproven shot.
- The daily shot keeps its existing pool, so its history stays valid.

Acceptance: every shot's stored solution makes it through `commit()` on each table setting; migration case per star value; a new run beats an old score; a discipline total adds up; page fit and contrast as other sheets.

## T6.4 career

Evidence: the same research, whose ten-season sims show every season's calendar names identical (49 of 49), no relegation ever, a 72% player promoted ten times in ten, a rival at 128-0, and 512,398 credits against a shop of 26,100.

All additive. Add `upgrade(S)` in `load`. `S.aw` stays 6 fields, `DISCS` append-only, `genPlayer`'s streams untouched, seed 99's fingerprint `dbfcdfc7` held.

In priority order. The research's item numbers:

1. Season review screen (`PAGES.review`), once per rollover.
2. Stakes: from tier 8, keep your card on a rolling two-year money list or go to the qualifier (`S.ml`); a title no longer auto-promotes above tier 7. **An old save starts its list at zero with a grace season**, so nobody is relegated by an upgrade.
3. Calendar variety: per-season host city and prestige swings, one-offs; ids stable, `S.ser` untouched, a new seed name, no new draws from `genSeries`.
4. Tour-card bug: `me()` overwrites `card` with `cardOf`. Trace it, expose `tourCard`, add a hub row.
5. Rivals: rank by closeness and tier gap, retire dead ones, cap challenges per season.
6. Sponsors: scale on the last 26 weeks, add renewals.
7. Performance rating `S.me.fr` on Fargo's scale (100 points is 2:1) and an odds line on the match card. Named generically: "FargoRate" is a trademark.
8. Money sink: cosmetic prestige tiers and venue ownership, `effect: 'none'`, purely cosmetic.
9. Press lines from `S.arch` and `S.h2h`; a one-year invitation list.

Acceptance: a check per item as named in the research; a pre-T6 save fixture loads and plays on; a ten-season sim shows calendars that differ, a cold player who loses their card, a top rival under 3:1; save under 260 KB at season 10.
