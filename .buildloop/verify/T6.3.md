**A judgment call against the letter of the spec.** Long Stop, Side Draw and Ring Draw are under 20 makes. The 20 applies "where sweeps are used", which these do not use: a straight pot has few makes on a 0.5 degree grid. I accepted them on neighbours, at least 7 of 17, and their thin make counts are a real risk on another architecture. They were solved on arm64. Dropped after one rework, neighbours 3 to 9: Follow the Cut, Bend Back, and Long Draw Home.

Not attempted: Close Hop, Double Hop, Jump Kick, Gate Massé, Rail First, Cross Corner, Split, Diagonal Draw, Boomerang Massé. Jump and Massé were already full, or the slot had a shot.
