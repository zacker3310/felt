How to play
Aim
Point with the mouse, or tap where the cue ball should go. Drag across the line to fine-tune; start the drag further from the cue ball for finer steps. ← → also aim (Shift coarse, Alt fine).
Shoot
Drag back along the line, away from the target, and let go: the further, the harder. Slide back to the start to cancel. Or drag the power bar and let go. Space shoots at the marked power, ↑ ↓ set it.
English
Move the blue tip on the cue ball: high for follow, low for draw, the sides for english. Double tap it or press R to centre.
In hand
Drag anywhere to slide the cue ball, or tap a spot. Let go to place it. You can pick it up again until you shoot.
Pause
The pause button or Esc. The game waits.
